# Arithmetic Trainer

A Zetamac-style mental math trainer that remembers the problems you miss and brings them back until you answer them fast.

## Try it

**[Open the trainer →](https://migueldaviddiaz.github.io/Arithmetic-Trainer/)**

It runs in your browser with nothing to install, and your progress stays on your device.

## How it works

The default game matches Zetamac: two minutes of addition, subtraction, multiplication and division, and each answer submits as soon as it's correct. If you get a problem wrong or take too long on it, it goes on your weak list, and part of every round is drawn from that list. A problem comes off the list once you solve it quickly three times in a row.

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in a browser. It's a single file with no dependencies.
